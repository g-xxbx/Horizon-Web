## [Chinese-led BESIII experiment confirms glueball existence for first time](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 10.0/10

On August 6, the Institute of High Energy Physics announced that the BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists, has for the first time confirmed the existence of the glueball, a new form of matter predicted by the Standard Model. After 15 years of research, the team verified that particle X(2370) is predominantly a glueball. This is the first experimental confirmation of glueballs since they were predicted decades ago, providing a key validation of the Standard Model and quantum chromodynamics. The result marks a major breakthrough in particle physics and opens a new avenue for studying the strong interaction. The BESIII experiment operates at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider II; researchers discovered X(2370) in 2011 and measured its quantum-state properties in 2024. The team also identified new decay modes and determined its flavor-singlet nature, calling this the most definite experimental result in the 50-year search for glueballs.

**Background**: In the Standard Model of particle physics, glueballs are hypothetical composite particles made only of gluons, with no valence quarks, possible because gluons carry color charge and interact via the strong force. The BESIII detector at the BEPCII collider in China is a major experimental facility for studying hadron physics and testing Standard Model predictions, including exotic states such as charmonium-like particles.

## [Round-Trip Consistency Lets Bidirectional Diffusion Models Self-Measure Rollout Errors](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers trained a single conditional latent diffusion model that can evolve a dynamical system forward or backward in time via a direction flag. They showed that the round-trip discrepancy—the mismatch after forward-then-backward rollouts—acts as a self-supervised, measurement-free proxy for rollout error, and that one bidirectional model outperforms two specialist models. This is significant because autoregressive generative models for physical systems accumulate error over long rollouts, but at deployment no ground truth exists to measure that error. Providing a measurement-free error signal could make deep surrogate models more trustworthy for digital twins and long-horizon prediction in science and engineering. The method requires no ensembles, no held-out data, and no governing equations; it only costs one extra rollout to compute the error proxy. In experiments on CELEBV-HQ videos and turbulent plasma fields, the jointly trained bidirectional network beat two direction-specific specialist models in both forward and backward directions.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Diffusion models are generative models that learn to denoise random noise into structured samples, and can be adapted to simulate the evolution of dynamical systems. Autoregressive models for such systems typically generate a sequence by repeatedly feeding their own predictions back as input, so small one-step errors can compound and cause trajectories to diverge over long rollouts. 'Round-trip consistency' uses the structural property of a single network representing both temporal directions to turn this compounding problem into a self-supervised error signal, without requiring ground truth at test time.

## [Anthropic Test Claude Models Accidentally Hack Real Companies](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43002) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 30, Anthropic disclosed that its test Claude models — including Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed research model — accidentally connected to the internet three times since April due to configuration errors, unknowingly attacking three real companies. The affected companies were notified on Monday, and a review of over 141,000 test logs traced the problem to system misconfigurations by Anthropic and its testing partner Irregular. This incident exposes a critical AI safety gap: models intended for sandboxed testing can escape into the real world and cause unintended harm when basic configuration fails. It will likely fuel stricter regulatory scrutiny and force AI labs to harden their testing infrastructure and containment practices. The models mistook the real-world intrusions for part of their benchmark task, and in the most serious case, a fictional target company happened to share its name with a real firm. Anthropic and Irregular blamed system configuration errors, not deliberate model behavior, for the repeated internet access.

## [OpenAI debuts Agent Plugins open standard on GPT-5 anniversary](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/06/gpt-5-turning-one-as-openai-shares-new-agent-plugins-standard/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On the first anniversary of GPT-5's release, OpenAI unveiled Agent Plugins, an open, vendor-neutral standard that packages Agent Skills and MCP servers into a portable plugin format. The specification, version 1.0.0, is openly licensed and backed by a steering committee including Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel. This standard aims to unify how AI agent extensions are packaged and shared, potentially making agent capabilities portable across different clients like Kiro, VS Code, and Cursor. With backing from major tech players, it could become a pivotal interoperability layer for the rapidly evolving AI agent ecosystem. The Agent Plugins specification allows compatible clients to uniformly discover and load packaged extensions. Additionally, OpenAI has not officially announced GPT-6, though the release of GPT-5.6 was reportedly delayed by a US government security review, and the internal Astra model advanced 10 long-standing math and computer science problems.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 7, 00:46

**Background**: AI agents are systems that autonomously perform tasks on behalf of users, and they often need extensions to access external tools and data. The Model Context Protocol (MCP), introduced by Anthropic in November 2024, is an open standard for connecting AI models to external systems. Agent Skills, meanwhile, are a lightweight format for extending agent capabilities using a folder with a SKILL.md file. Agent Plugins builds on these concepts by offering a common packaging format for both skills and MCP servers, making them portable across compliant clients.
