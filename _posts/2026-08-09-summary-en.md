## [Generative Design of Viable Bacteriophage Genomes Using Evo 1 and Evo 2](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate whole-genome sequences of bacteriophages based on the lytic phage ΦX174, and experimental testing produced 16 viable novel phages. This represents the first generative design of complete bacteriophage genomes using genome language models. This breakthrough demonstrates that large-scale language models can generate functional whole genomes, not just short motifs, opening new avenues in synthetic biology and phage engineering. It could accelerate the design of custom bacteriophages for therapy, biocontrol, and research, while offering insights into the evolution and viability of genomic sequences. The AI-generated genomes were designed with realistic genetic architectures and desirable host tropism, using E. coli phage ΦX174 as the template. Out of hundreds of synthetic genomes distinct from natural sequences, 16 phages were experimentally confirmed viable, showing substantial evolutionary novelty.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models such as Evo 1 and Evo 2 are deep learning models trained on raw DNA sequences at single-nucleotide resolution. Evo 2 is a 40-billion-parameter model with a 1-megabase context length, trained on over 9 trillion nucleotides. Bacteriophages are viruses that infect bacteria, and ΦX174 is a well-known lytic phage that infects E. coli. Host tropism refers to the specificity of a pathogen for particular host species or tissues, which the researchers aimed to control when generating new phage genomes.

## [World's largest single AI computing facility begins operations in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group announced that its 'Envision Ulanqab Xinghe Base' officially entered production. The facility is described as the world's largest single AI computing center, covering 120,000 square meters, supporting million-GPU parallel computing, with a planned total capacity of 2GW and over 80% green power. This milestone underscores how AI infrastructure is scaling up both in compute capacity and energy efficiency, and it advances China's 'East Data, West Computing' strategy. The project could provide a replicable model for domestic AI compute clusters and help reduce computing costs in the region. Ulanqab is one of China's eight national hub nodes for the 'East Data, West Computing' project, located about 240 km from Beijing with a data transmission latency of only 4.2 milliseconds. Electricity prices for data centers there are roughly 50% lower than in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, and the base is the first flagship project of Envision's 'Gobi Mission' plan.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 05:06

**Background**: China's 'East Data, West Computing' (东数西算) initiative, launched around 2021, aims to redirect eastern computing demand to western regions with abundant renewable energy and cooler climates, building national hub nodes in places like Inner Mongolia. In the era of large AI models, data centers are increasingly seen as 'Token factories' that consume electricity and data to produce tokens, the fundamental units AI models process; as a result, token output per watt has become a key measure of computing efficiency.
